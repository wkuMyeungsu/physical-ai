## Brand & Style

The design system embodies an organic modern aesthetic tailored for physical wellness technology. It rejects the cold, sterile, hyper-futuristic tropes of traditional AI consumer hardware in favor of warm, tactile, and living-room-ready ergonomics. The visual tone bridges clinical orthopedic credibility with approachable, compassionate daily habit coaching.

Key attributes:
- **Warm & Domestic:** Feels at home alongside ceramic mugs, linen desks, and wooden furnishings rather than sterile server racks.
- **Empathetic & Non-Judgmental:** Correcting posture and mouth-breathing should feel supportive and empowering, never punitive or clinical.
- **Calmly Technical:** Communicates edge computing, on-device neural processing, and zero-cloud privacy without technical intimidation or sensor paranoia.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in calming natural pigments balanced with high-contrast, readable slate typography.

- **Primary (`#3B6E59` - Restorative Sage):** Serves as the key signifier of health, balanced alignment, active habit streaks, and primary CTAs. Evokes biological harmony and calm focus.
- **Secondary (`#D97757` - Warm Terracotta / Clay):** Used for corrective feedback states (e.g., mouth-breathing alert, forward head posture notice), interactive accents, badge highlights, and gentle warnings. Offers urgency without anxiety.
- **Tertiary (`#5C768D` - Slate Blue / Edge AI Accent):** Represents privacy, security, and local on-device processing. Applied to security badges, architectural flow lines, and neutral interactive states.
- **Neutral Base & Canvas:**
  - Base canvas uses `#FBF9F5` (Warm Linen Ivory).
  - Raised card surfaces use `#FFFFFF` (Pure Porcelain) and `#F4EFEA` (Warm Stone Tint).
  - Typography and high-contrast outlines anchor onto `#1E293B` (Deep Slate Charcoal) for optimal legibility and grounded presence.

## Typography

Typography relies on `Plus Jakarta Sans` across all tiers for its blend of geometric clarity and humanist curvature. This ensures high legibility across multilingual body copy (English and Korean hangul pairings) while maintaining a friendly, warm profile.

- **Display & Headlines:** Use tight negative letter spacing with medium-heavy weights (`700` and `800`) to anchor landing page value propositions and section hooks.
- **Body:** Kept generous in line height (`1.5` to `1.55`) to facilitate calm, effortless reading across narrative health explanations.
- **Metric Counters (`counter-xl`):** Dedicated display style for ROI metrics, habit completion rates, and physical tilt degrees, maintaining high visual punch without appearing clinical.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a responsive 12-column fluid grid system on desktop, collapsing to 8 columns on tablet and 4 columns on mobile.

- **Breakpoints:**
  - Desktop: `>= 1024px` (12 columns, `margin: 2rem`, max-width `1280px` centered).
  - Tablet: `768px – 1023px` (8 columns, `margin: 1.5rem`).
  - Mobile: `< 768px` (4 columns, `margin-sm: 1.25rem`).
- **Rhythm & Whitespace:** Generous vertical section padding (`space-xl` scaled up to `5rem`–`7rem` on desktop sections) separates key product narratives (Problem Definition -> Hardware Sensor Tour -> Edge Privacy Model -> Habit Character Previews -> Order CTA). Content breathes naturally without claustrophobic framing.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through warm, diffused ambient shadows and gentle surface-container shifts rather than high-contrast stark drops or reflective glassmorphism.

- **Ambient Cast Shadows:** Shadows are warm-tinted using the deep slate neutral mixed with amber/stone hues:
  - Low Tier (Resting cards, badges): `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.05), 0 1px 4px -1px rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.03)`
  - Medium Tier (Character preview cards, interactive feature blocks): `0 12px 24px -6px rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.07), 0 4px 8px -2px rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.03)`
  - High Tier (Floating CTAs, sticky habit modals): `0 20px 40px -10px rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.1), 0 8px 16px -4px rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.04)`
- **Low-Contrast Tonal Outlines:** Surface borders avoid harsh grays. Use a 1px border tinted in warm clay-stone: `rgba(30, 41, 59, 0.08)` or `rgba(59, 110, 89, 0.12)` when highlighting active or valid states.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a grounded, ergonomic rounded language (`roundedness: 2`). 

- Standard containers and cards feature `1rem` (16px) corner radii (`rounded-lg`), mirroring the curved matte edges of physical ergonomic hardware.
- Hero feature blocks and character habit showcases scale up to `1.5rem` (24px) corner radii (`rounded-xl`).
- Buttons, chips, status pills, and edge AI badges employ full pill rounding (`9999px`) to invite touch and reinforce the friendly, human-centric demeanor.
